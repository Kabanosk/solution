## Goal
`backend/cfo/forecast/features.py`: read transactions/invoices from the DB, return daily net cash flow with calendar features and monthly cost per team (`tasks/T014-forecast-features.md`; D-002, D-009). T015 trains XGBoost on it.

## What was done
- Added dependency `pandas>=2.2` to `backend/pyproject.toml` (+ `uv.lock`; resolved to pandas 4.x). T015 will touch the same files when adding xgboost: expect a trivial merge/relock.
- `cfo/forecast/features.py`, pure functions on DataFrames plus read-only DB wrappers:
  - `fetch_transactions(conn=None)`, `fetch_invoices(conn=None)`: `conn` is an optional psycopg connection (own schema in tests), default `DATABASE_URL`.
  - `daily_cash_flow(tx, start, end, exclude_categories)`: per calendar day `net_cash_flow` (target), `inflow`, `outflow`, `revenue`, `n_transactions`; no gaps, quiet days are 0.
  - `calendar_features(dates, shutdown_days, due_days)`: works for any dates incl. the future. Columns: `weekday, is_weekend, month, quarter, day_of_month, day_of_year, week_of_year, days_to_month_end, is_month_start, is_month_end, is_public_holiday` (German federal, Easter computed), `shutdown_flag`, `payroll_due, rent_due, tax_due, social_security_due, utilities_due` (all int).
  - `infer_shutdown_days(tx, min_run=7)`: runs of >= 7 consecutive zero-revenue calendar days, stored as (month, day) pairs so they project onto future years. A run touching the last observed day is ignored (data may just end there). Gaps shorter than 7 days are not shutdowns.
  - `infer_due_days(tx)`: days of month with a booking in >= 3 distinct months per fixed-cost category (payroll 28, rent 1, tax 10 and 15, social security 27, utilities 10 on the seeded data), fallback to defaults. Day clipped to month end.
  - `daily_features(tx, end=...)` / `load_daily_features(conn, end=...)`: history rows with cash-flow columns, future rows (up to `end`, e.g. 2026-07-31) with calendar features only and NaN cash flow.
  - `monthly_cost_by_team(tx, invoices, by_category=False, include_unassigned=True)` / `load_monthly_cost_by_team`: columns `month, team_id[, category], cost` (positive EUR), months zero-filled. Bank lines (except revenue, partner_investment) count in the booking month; invoices count in the issue month only when `transaction_id` is empty (linked ones are already bank lines, so no double counting). Costs without a team (tax, social security) go to team `company`.
- Tests `backend/tests/test_forecast_features.py` (12): synthetic fixture (Jan-Aug 2025 with a 20-day shutdown) for cash flow, shutdown inference and its edge cases, due days, holidays, future June 2026, cost aggregation and double-count guard; one DB test seeds `t014_test` schema from `data/generated` (skips without Postgres or generated data), drops it afterwards. Public schema untouched.

## Next
- Real shutdown in the data is all of June 2025 (30 days, 1-30 June, zero revenue every day), not 3 weeks. `shutdown_flag` therefore marks the whole of June 2026. T015 can still learn it from `shutdown_flag`/`month`/`day_of_year`.
- `shutdown_flag` is derived from the same history it labels, so on training rows it perfectly separates the dip; it is the seasonal projection that carries it to 2026. With one year of history cross-validation by year is impossible; T015 should evaluate on a held-out slice of other months or by backtest.
- `partner_investment` (the 12 unconverted Swiss tranches) is in `net_cash_flow` as printed; use `exclude_categories=("partner_investment",)` to drop it, T015 decides.
- Bank-only cash flow: ads, cloud and the oven are on the company card, so they are in the monthly team costs (invoices) but not in daily cash flow. Unpaid December invoices (issued, not yet linked) also show up as cost in their issue month.
- No lag/rolling features: T015 can add them on top of `daily_features` (they would need recursive forecasting for the horizon).
