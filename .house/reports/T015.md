## What was done
- Added deps `xgboost`, `scikit-learn` (XGBRegressor needs it; wheels install fine on the pinned Python 3.12, no tree fallback needed) and optional extra `mlflow` (`uv sync --extra mlflow`); `models/` added to `.gitignore`. `uv.lock` conflict with main resolved by taking main's file and re-running `uv lock`.
- `cfo/forecast/model.py`:
  - `train(transactions)` / `train_from_db(conn)`: XGBRegressor (200 trees, depth 3, lr 0.05, min_child_weight 3, deterministic) on the 22 calendar features of `daily_features`; target = daily net cash flow. A 60-day time holdout gives `holdout_mae` / `rmse` / `naive_mae` / `total_error` (stored in `model.metrics`). One year of history cannot be cross-validated by year (the shutdown happens once).
  - `ForecastModel`: `predict(dates)`, `feature_importances()` (gain share, sums to 1), `contributions(dates)` (per-day SHAP in EUR), `explain(start, end, top)` -> dict with predicted net, baseline, top drivers and importances (this is what T016 should hand to the LLM), `save(path)` / `load(path)`: booster JSON + `<file>.meta.json` (shutdown days, due days, metrics, params, trained_until). Default `models/liquidity_xgb.json`, override `FORECAST_MODEL_PATH`.
  - `liquidity_forecast(days, start_balance=None, *, model, transactions, conn, scheduled_payments)` -> DataFrame indexed by `date` with `predicted_net`, `scheduled`, `net`, `balance`; `attrs` has `start_balance` and `last_known_date`. First day = day after the last booking; default start balance = running sum of all bookings (the statement has no opening balance; the organizer's chart starts at 0 too). Without `model` it loads the saved one or trains and saves on first use (`get_model`). `lowest_balance(df)` helper.
  - MLflow: `log_to_mlflow_if_configured` imports `mlflow` only if `MLFLOW_TRACKING_URI` is set; logs params, metrics, booster and `feature_importances.json`; missing package or tracking error only logs a warning.
- **Decision: `partner_investment` is excluded from the learned target and added as a known scheduled payment.** The statement says "Tranche n/12", the last (12/12) is 15 Dec 2025, so the schedule ends. Learned from one year it would be projected into 2026 and hide 4.2k a month that is freed. `partner_tranche_schedule(tx)` derives the remaining tranches from the bookings (11 booked by end of November -> 15 Dec 2025 remains; 12 booked -> none). Pass `scheduled_payments=` to override or `[]` for none. The amount stays as printed (-4166.67, unconverted CHF), consistent with the balance.
- `backend/tests/test_forecast_model.py` (15 tests): synthetic fixture (tranche exclusion and schedule, forecast shape, `start_balance` shift, scheduled override, save/load round trip, `get_model`, importances/SHAP additivity/explain, MLflow off/on with a fake module, missing mlflow) and the organizer's statement (Jan-Nov via `load_bank_csv`, skipped without the CSV). ## Tested and did not work
- **The balance does not go below zero in June 2026 when starting from the end-of-November balance**, so the requested `min balance in June 2026 < 0` cannot be asserted. Data fact, not a model flaw: the data has no opening balance (cumulative from 0), Dec-May is net positive and tranche 12/12 ends, so the balance is ~60k going into June and troughs near 45k (min of June 45.6k). What the data supports, asserted in the test: June 2026 net < -10k (predicted -14.1k) while every other month is positive; balance drops > 10k within June (-14.1k, minus no tranche); the deepest drawdown of the whole horizon lies in the June shutdown; a 55k lower buffer goes negative in June (stress test, not base case). **Consequence for the demo:** the T018 stub (balance -20k on 27 June 2026, start 42k) does not match what the model says. For a credit-line story the coordinator should decide what the agent compares against (a safety buffer such as ~1-2 months of fixed costs ~17.7k/month, or a lower start balance); it is a threshold decision, not a data one. Suggest raising it as a Q.
- A deeper model (depth 4-5, 300-400 trees) fit holdout worse (MAE 245-389 vs 207); picked depth 3.
- Model is trained on 11 months, so December is extrapolated (predicted +15.2k vs real +13.5k without the tranche; fine).
