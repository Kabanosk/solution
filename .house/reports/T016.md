## Goal
`detect_gaps`, `forecast_with`, `propose_credit_line`, `propose_investment` per D-013 (`tasks/T016-liquidity-gaps.md`).

## What was done
- New `backend/cfo/forecast/liquidity.py`:
  - `default_buffer(tx)` / `fixed_costs_monthly(tx)`: the most expensive calendar month of payroll, rent, tax, social security, utilities. On the statement: **17,730.00 EUR** (June; the D-013 "about 17.7k"). Override with `buffer=`.
  - `forecast_with(extra_outflows=[...], days=240, start_balance=None, ...)`: `fm.liquidity_forecast` plus extra payments (`Outflow(on, amount, label)`, `(date, amount[, label])` tuples or dicts; positive EUR; must fall in the horizon, else ValueError). Adds column `what_if`; `attrs` carries `default_buffer` and `outflows`. Partner tranche schedule is kept.
  - `detect_gaps(forecast, buffer=None, transactions=None)` -> `LiquidityGap` list (start, end, lowest date/balance, shortfall, recovery date; Decimal EUR). A day at exactly the buffer is not a gap.
  - `propose_credit_line(gap | gaps, evidence)`: amount = deepest shortfall x 1.2 rounded up to 1,000; period = 14 days before the first gap to 30 days after the last recovers; `month` = month the deepest gap starts in. Classified with `classify(action_type="request_credit_line")` -> APPROVAL (a CEO `Rule` can still lower it; tested).
  - `find_surplus(forecast, buffer)` + `propose_investment(surplus)`: only when there is no gap and a weak (negative net) complete month lies ahead. Invests at the balance peak before the weak month; amount = (lowest balance from that day to horizon end - buffer) rounded down to 500, so June (and everything after) stays at or above the buffer. `make_investment` -> APPROVAL. Default instrument "money market fund".
  - Reasons are built in code (numbers never from the LLM): closed weeks from `model.shutdown_days`, revenue drop vs the median month, fixed costs, planned what-if outflows, net flow and top SHAP drivers of the weak month from `ForecastModel.explain` with feature names translated ("the summer shutdown (-15,758 EUR)"). Raw `drivers` and `importances` are on the proposal. Example (30k oven on 15 Mar): "June 2026: the restaurant is closed about 4 weeks, so revenue drops by about 47,600 EUR against a normal month, while fixed costs of 17,730 EUR per month continue. Planned in this scenario: oven 30,000 EUR on 2026-03-15. The model expects a net cash flow of -14,113 EUR in June 2026, mainly because of the summer shutdown (-15,758 EUR); ... The balance is predicted to fall to 12,527 EUR on 2026-07-02, 5,203 EUR below the safety buffer of 17,730 EUR ..."
  - `review_liquidity(extra_outflows, ...)`: forecast, gaps, one proposal (credit line if gaps, else investment if surplus). `LiquidityProposal.to_event()` gives the METRIC event for the agent.
- **Minimal change to `backend/cfo/agent/graph.py` (T005):** the `propose_credit_line` node now reads optional `action_type` (`request_credit_line` default | `make_investment`) and `why` from the METRIC payload. With `why` it uses the text verbatim instead of an LLM explanation (the numbers are already computed); without it behaviour is unchanged (existing T005 tests pass). Summary/step texts per action type are in `LIQUIDITY_ACTIONS`. No change to the approval flow.

## Tested and did not work
- **D-013's example does not hold on the real forecast.** "Oven 25k in March" does not break the buffer in June: the lowest balance becomes 17,527 EUR on 2 July, only 203 EUR under the 17,730 buffer (covered by a 1,000 EUR line, so technically a gap). A clear breach needs about 28k+: **30k in March** gives a deepest gap of 5,203 EUR (credit line 7,000 EUR, period 1 Mar - 5 Aug) plus three tiny March/April gaps of 0.3-1.8k (payroll days right after the purchase). The demo should say "30k" (or buy in May: single gap 28 Jun - 5 Jul, same 7,000 EUR). Tests pin both numbers.
- The June trough is on **2 July** (payroll 28 June, rent 1 July with no revenue), so the gap starts 28 June and ends 5 July, not "in June". The proposal's `month` is the gap start month (2026-06).
- Investment base case on the statement: 24,500 EUR from 26 May (peak) keeps the balance at 18,027 EUR or more. The binding day is 2 July, not June, which is why the sizing looks at the whole horizon after the peak.
