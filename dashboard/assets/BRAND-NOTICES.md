# Brand artwork

`google-g.png` is Google's official multicolor G artwork, downloaded from
https://developers.google.com/identity/images/g-logo.png for the Google sign-in button.
Google and its logo are trademarks of Google LLC. The artwork is not recolored or
modified; it is displayed beside the Google sign-in label according to the
[Google sign-in branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
