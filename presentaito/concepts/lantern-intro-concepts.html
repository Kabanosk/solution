Lantern: opening slide concepts

Three ways to open the pitch with a real lantern bringing light into the company's dark corners. Each runs about 10 seconds and loops. Pick one and it goes on the first slide and at the start of the video.

Concept A

The search

A lantern swings through a dark pile of company stuff: invoices, receipts, chats, emails, laptops. Whatever its light touches turns crisp and gets a green check. At the end the light floods the screen white and the name appears, which hands off straight into the white deck.

Concept B

Lights on across the company

The lantern sits in the middle of a dark company map. One by one, warm beams reach the bank, the invoicing system, each laptop and Slack, and every node lights up with what Lantern found there. It shows the interconnection and the brand idea in one shot.

Concept C

The dark ledger

A lantern lowers on a string over a ledger of unexplained payments. As it swings down the list, each row lights up and resolves: matched, cancelled, or "asked Anna". A counter climbs to 312 of 312 explained. The most finance-specific of the three.
