Scout

Your autonomous CFO

Scout is your autonomous CFO. It notices changes across the business, checks what already exists, and handles the next step when it can. It asks a person only when it needs one. Let’s start with a charge on a company card.

Scout
Scout can start with a payment, an email, a new invoice or a spend alert. It checks the system of record first. If that doesn’t explain the event, it looks in authorized files, mail and existing conversations. [Trace the solid line.] If the evidence is enough, it takes the allowed action and records the source. [Point to the dashed branch.] Reading a conversation is not the same as starting one. It sends a new Slack DM only if someone needs to supply information or make a decision.
